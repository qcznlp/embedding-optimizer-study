# Actual complete weight-to-retrieval numerical readout

This local plan is written after the complete retrieval scores and weight
measurements were visible. It is not a new preregistration. It executes the
existing September 3/6 definitions, retaining all original nine features and
the separately declared five exact sensitivity features, all four held-dose
folds and every undefined result. No feature or outcome selection is introduced.

Inputs are the previously anonymously recovered immutable weight snapshot,
the accepted complete sixty-state trajectory tables, and the complete raw-score
recovery index. Join every state by run identity, retained step and original
checkpoint seal, not just a human-readable run name. Check both branches against
their raw checkpoint rows and immutable transport manifest. Reuse the existing
checkpoint-backed geometry acceptance; do not rerun models, SVDs or retrieval.

Execute only unchanged, hash-bound pure calculation AST nodes from the original
source. No contract object, released-source record or upstream acceptance is
fabricated. The complete formal consumers remain uncalled and unadmitted.
Recipe views describe authentic saved recipes; they are not training configs.

The calculation keeps exact binary64-to-rational OLS, the eight-column baseline,
one additional feature at a time, 45 train / 15 test rows per fold, numerical
resolution and undefined policies, and the lower pooled MSE plus at least three
improved folds rule. Exact features do not replace approximate measurements.

Verify the actual predictions with an independent full augmented-matrix rational
OLS implementation, verify state/feature coverage, and replay with copied inputs
and source. Archive successful and failed attempts separately. Report predictive
support as one-seed fixed-grid association, never significance, mediation,
retrieval-useful dimensions, a new optimizer-quality verdict or formal release.

Only new local readout files and a dated handoff are in scope. No original
numerical/dispatch/protocol file, model process, protected helper, historical
controller, manuscript, GitHub state or remote artifact is modified.
