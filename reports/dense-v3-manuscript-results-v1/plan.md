are visible. It changes neither the scientific design nor a numerical definition.

content-bound numerical reports. Reuse the unchanged original scientific renderers
and retain their raw output. Keep all three primary and all three secondary
contrasts, all nine original and five exact weight predictors, all nine functional

without choosing a preferred comparator or rewriting an original finding.

process must reconstruct every generated byte without producer-directory fallback.

flags; inspect the rendered result pages. Input authentication and formatting
are distinct from fresh model admission, upstream numerical replay, GPU resume,
complete factorial inference, manuscript installation and source publication.

alter the frozen publication contracts, or call an old admission entry with a
substitute identity. The final complete-paper consumer and release gates remain
required. No implementation incident belongs in the generated scientific text.
