# Complete exact-sensitivity reconstruction — bounded engineering evidence

This work connects the separately declared five-feature exact sensitivity to the
complete original/functional/publication reconstruction. It does not replace any
of the nine original weight features, alter a frozen numerical kernel or protocol,
admit a primary run, install manuscript results or authorize a release.

Three new package modules provide exact primitive readback, actual checkpoint-backed
archive authoring, and a full local-role numerical reader. The latter first invokes
the complete original/functional publication reader, then reconstructs all exact
geometry tables and all seven exact-bridge tables from those fresh inputs. Both
complete authoring gathers are required by the real archive builder; simulation is
confined to a separate fixture helper.

## What is and is not reconstructed

The portable reader recomputes complete-spectrum scalar definitions, numerical
signal rank/cutoff status, checkpoint aggregates and exact projector overlaps. It
retains the old nonzero-matrix denominators and does not fill undefined measurements.
The two measurement layers must agree on exact zero presence and on the same saved
displacement norm within the already declared full-SVD reference tolerances. The
same original FP64 weight norms feed both checkpoint summaries.

Saved singular spectra, bases, SVD reconstruction residuals and weight norms remain
authenticated measurements. Their raw checkpoint origin is established by actual
authoring, not re-established by transport. The independent real readback does not
promote short diagnostic identities into the twelve-run primary population.

The new full fixture preserves all non-source bytes from the accepted publication
fixture. It adds explicit hypothetical full-native-shape exact spectra and coordinate
bases, retaining the same recorded displacement norms/zero patterns; these are not
newly measured or internally admitted model trajectories. It rebuilds the complete
current source closure and adds exact roles under `provenance/`. Original producer
locations remain lexical and cannot be opened by the cold reader.

The seven original publication and eight new exact-output mutation controls reuse
only the same cold invocation's fresh expected results. They are not fifteen extra
full raw reconstructions or fifteen fresh-process controls. Both completed cold
runs passed these controls after their respective full raw reconstruction.

No new scientific result, final manuscript, clean remote release, formal run,
controller transition, commit/push or HF deletion is supplied. The strict active-v3
paper consumer, exact-result manuscript presentation, routed factorial, reviewed
source/runtime transition and real primary experiment remain required.
