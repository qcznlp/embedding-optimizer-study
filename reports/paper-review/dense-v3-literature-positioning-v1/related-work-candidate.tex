\section{Related Work}

Dense dual encoders rank a corpus through vector similarity. BEIR tests transfer across
retrieval tasks \citep{thakur2021beir}; DenseOn supplies the pretrained checkpoint and open
adaptation recipe \citep{sourty2026denseon}. HIL combines isotropic and anisotropic
representations in late-interaction retrieval \citep{kim2024hil}, motivating relevance-sensitive
evaluation rather than maximizing a spread statistic alone.

\citet{takeshita2025dimensions} study random and single-coordinate removal in text embeddings.
\citet{wu2026select} learn query-specific dimension selection from positive--negative
alignment. Relevance-sensitive dimension importance is therefore not itself our contribution.
We track finite deletion sensitivities along optimizer trajectories and test whether their
native-coordinate differences survive shared rotations and predict full-corpus retrieval.

Muon scales well in language-model pretraining, and NorMuon adds neuron-wise adaptation
\citep{liu2025muon,li2025normuon}. Associative-memory and imbalance studies connect spectral
updates to more balanced learning \citep{wang2025tailmemory,vasudeva2025spectral}; feature-transfer
benefits have also been reported \citep{ruan2026features}. These are functional explanations,
not just descriptions of an update rule. Yet spectral descent can lose useful simplicity
bias \citep{dragutinovic2026simplicity}, and optimizer switching can disrupt pretrained
knowledge \citep{qu2026finetune,liu2026consistency}. Spectral correction, input covariance,
and layer-dependent geometry offer further qualifications
\citep{pang2026htmuon,du2026newtonmuon,massena2026schatten}.

Basis and scale require separate care. Outlier-safe pretraining combines Muon with architectural
changes \citep{park2025outlier}; parameter-gauge equivariance is distinct from our post-encoding
rotations \citep{singh2026basis}. Hyperball connects weight and update norms to angular dynamics
\citep{wen2026hyperball}. Our initial fixed-probe calibration does not match entire trajectories.
We ask which reached-state measurements predict held-out-rate retrieval beyond the training
recipe, then separately cross fixed source states with reset continuation rules. Neither a
spectral fingerprint nor a basis-sensitive coordinate effect alone establishes a retrieval
mechanism.
