\paragraph{Candidate-breadth uncertainty and paired prevalence.}
The prerequisite width-7 bridge failed: independently padded scoring did not reproduce the legacy packed validation outputs (maximum error 8.286419). On the padded path, the Muon-family high-dose advantage is already absent at width 7, and widening to 2,048 candidates does not produce the required joint reversal. Missing-candidate coverage therefore does not explain the observed shortlist--corpus gap. Exact endpoint intervals are Muon loss/margin deltas move from +0.17706 [+0.08924, +0.27961]/-0.00667 [-0.01222, -0.00102] to +0.10865 [-0.06652, +0.28457]/+0.00097 [-0.00443, +0.00638]; NorMuon loss/margin deltas move from +0.19077 [+0.06092, +0.33202]/-0.00457 [-0.01062, +0.00139] to +0.11576 [-0.07579, +0.30990]/-0.00096 [-0.00692, +0.00494]. Intervals use 50,000 resamples, independently preserving each of the seven 32-query source strata; they are descriptive and do not enter the frozen support rule.

\paragraph{Descriptive paired prevalence.} The earliest retained width at which both mean contrasts favor the retrieval-optimal $3\!\times\!10^{-4}$ dose is Muon: 7 negatives; paired high-dose win fractions (loss/margin) 43.8\%/42.9\% at width 7 to 48.7\%/49.1\% at width 2,048; NorMuon: 7 negatives; paired high-dose win fractions (loss/margin) 47.8\%/47.3\% at width 7 to 51.8\%/51.8\% at width 2,048. This is a crossing on the six-point frozen grid, not an estimated threshold; the curves need not be monotone.
