# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want this project to be a respectful, harassment-free place for people of every background and
level of experience. Contributors, maintainers, and users are expected to communicate in good faith,
focus criticism on the work, and make room for questions and disagreement.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and specific when reviewing code, experiments, and scientific claims.
- Disclose limitations, failed runs, conflicts of interest, and protocol changes that affect
  comparability.
- Respect privacy and do not publish another person's credentials, private data, or correspondence.
- Credit prior work and contributions accurately.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized conduct, personal attacks, deliberate
misrepresentation of results, and publication of private information without permission are not
acceptable in project spaces.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through the contact route on their GitHub
profile. Do not open a public issue containing sensitive personal information. Maintainers may edit
or remove contributions, comments, or other participation that violates this policy and may
temporarily or permanently restrict participation. Reports will be handled as confidentially as
practical, and retaliation against a reporter is not permitted.

This policy applies to repository discussions, issues, pull requests, review threads, and project
events where someone is representing the project.
